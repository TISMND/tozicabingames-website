The game

Syntherion is an arcade game built song by song. Every song is its own game, made around one synthwave track licensed from the artist who made it.

Your guns fire on the music. The world, the enemies, the mission and even the way you play come from the song: a night drive toward a ringed planet, a fight with a mech on a green grid, a freeway after dark.

Pick a song, play it, and find its artist: every song links to their Bandcamp, Spotify and Instagram.

Each game, from its song

  • Environment: shapes, colors and hazards fit the song's vibe.
  • Weapons: each instrument and melody drives its own gun.
  • Enemies: their behavior and attacks match the song's character.
  • Objectives: the mission is drawn from what the song is about.
