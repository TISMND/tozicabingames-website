Quiet World

Everyone has disappeared.

A journaling app and idle game, in development for iPhone and Android.

The game

Quiet World is a journaling app and an idle game. You start alone where you really are, on your own street, and from there the whole world is open: pick anywhere on Earth, a landmark or a far city, and set off on foot.

The walk goes on while the app is closed. Check in when you like and you'll be somewhere new; the app tells you when you arrive. Stop at the shops along the way for food and water, and rest when you need to.

Keep a journal of the road, a page and a picture for wherever you are. And leave notes behind: every player walks the same world, and anyone who passes that spot can read what you wrote. You may never meet another player. You might.

Features

  • The whole world, from your own street: real roads and real places, anywhere on Earth.
  • An idle game: your walk carries on while the app is closed.
  • A journal of the road, every page with a picture of where you were.
  • Notes for other players, left where you stand and read by walking there.
  • Peaceful first: no death, no enemies. Just keep yourself fed, watered and rested.
